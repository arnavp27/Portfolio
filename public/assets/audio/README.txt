GARAGE AUDIO

After Rain is an original 48-second procedural ambient cue composed for Arnav Patil's portfolio. Rebuild the PCM source with scripts/compose-garage-music.mjs, then encode with FFmpeg libvorbis.

All computer cues, physical keyboard sounds, footsteps, fans, rain, and city traffic are synthesized in src/lib/garage/. No film, commercial game, or operating-system recordings are distributed with this repository. static.png is locally generated noise.

Optional rights-cleared recordings can be mapped in src/lib/garage/audio-clips.ts. Null entries use the built-in synthesized cues. See docs/assets.md.
